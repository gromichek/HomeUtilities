Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Summary

HomeUtilities has no servers and no user accounts of its own. The developer does not collect, receive, store or share any data of the app's users. There is no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Data stored by the app

Everything the user enters (properties, meters, readings, bills, payments, photos and documents, settings) is stored only on the user's own device. Backup files are created on the device and saved wherever the user chooses.

Google account and Google Drive (optional sync)

If the user connects a Google account in Settings → Sync, the app requests these permissions only:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata — to create, read and delete the app's own files in the hidden app data folder of the user's Google Drive. The app cannot see any other files in the user's Drive.
  • openid and email — to show which Google account is connected.

The sync files (the user's changes, snapshots of the data, photos and documents) are stored in the user's own Google Drive and are transferred directly between the user's devices and Google. They are not sent to the developer or to anyone else. The access tokens are stored only on the user's device.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data

  • Data on a device is deleted by uninstalling the app (or with "Delete all data" in the app).
  • The sync data in Google Drive is deleted in Google Drive: Settings → Manage apps → HomeUtilities → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • The app's access to the Google account is removed with "Disconnect" in the app or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

The app is not directed at children.
